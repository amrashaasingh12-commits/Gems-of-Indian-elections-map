# Gems of Indian Elections

*A public archive of reported SIR discrepancies and electoral irregularities in India.*

## About the Archive

Gems of Indian Elections is an independent, publicly accessible archive that aims to collect, organise, and document publicly available evidence concerning reported electoral irregularities, SIR discrepancies or anything else related to elections across India.

The archive seeks to make such material easier to locate, examine and revisit, particularly where original online sources may subsequently become inaccessible.

## Scope of the Archive

The archive currently organises evidence into four categories:

1. **SIR Exclusions** — Material relating to exclusions and other issues arising from Special Intensive Revision processes.
2. **Polling Issues** — Reported irregularities occurring during polling.
3. **Local Electoral Roll Problems** — Issues concerning electoral rolls and voter registration at the local level.
4. **Others** — Electoral issues not covered by the preceding categories.

## Sources and Methodology

The archive collects material from publicly available sources, including:

- News reports
- X (formerly Twitter)
- Instagram

Each archival entry is assigned a unique identification number and includes relevant information such as its title, state or Union Territory, issue category, source type, year and a link to the corresponding archival post.

The archive primarily collects existing publicly reported material rather than conducting independent investigations.

Inclusion of material does not constitute independent verification of the claims made in the original source. However, the source is checked to the best of personal knowledge like concerned evidence (photos or videos) regarding the discrpenacy and the person sharing it.

## Archive Database

The archive uses a central JSON database (`entries.json`) to maintain its catalogue of entries.

Both the interactive map and the advanced search interface retrieve their information from this database.

The number of archived entries reflects the number of records collected and does not necessarily represent the number of distinct electoral incidents.

## Limitations

The archive is not an exhaustive record of electoral issues in India.

The availability of material depends on public reporting and access to original sources. Some links may become inaccessible over time.

Descriptions and allegations contained in source material should not automatically be treated as independently established facts.

## Project Status

This is an ongoing project. Additional entries and improvements to the archive will be incorporated over time.
